# Data provenance and licensing

What each input in this repository is, where it came from, and what you may do
with it.

Short version: the prompts are ours except for 130 rows from FLEURS, which are
CC BY 4.0 and attributed below. The audio is not here at all. The **model** is
the restrictive part — it is non-commercial.

## Prompt sets (`prompts/`)

| Set | Rows | Origin | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| `indic22_bench.jsonl` | 2,100 | **Generated by Rumik AI** for this benchmark. Not extracted from any external corpus. | Rumik AI. Published here to make the numbers reproducible. |
| `curated_hard_50.jsonl` | 1,050 | Generated with Google Gemini, then curated. Deliberately hard: every row stresses digit sequences. | Output of a generative model, governed by the Google generative-AI terms accepted at generation time. |
| `heldout_v1.jsonl` | 180 | **130 rows from FLEURS** (tagged `fleurs`) + 50 rows from `indic22_bench`. | The FLEURS rows are **CC BY 4.0** — reuse permitted with attribution. See below. |

The `wiki`, `book`, `news`, `conv`, `alexa`, `bb`, `umang` and `proper_noun`
tags on `indic22_bench` are **register labels**, not sources. They record the
style a prompt was written in — encyclopedic, literary, conversational, a voice
assistant query — because a TTS benchmark needs to span registers. They do not
mean the text was taken from Wikipedia, a book, or a news site.

### Attribution required: FLEURS

corpus, released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Audio

**No audio is in this repository, and none should be added.**

The vendor comparison synthesised audio through the commercial APIs of
ElevenLabs, Sarvam, Cartesia, Smallest.ai, Google (Gemini) and xAI (Grok). Most
TTS providers' terms restrict redistribution of generated audio, so republishing
it is not ours to do.

What is published instead is the *measurement*: `results/vendor_analysis.json`
holds the scores, and `results/vendor_config.json` holds the exact request
configuration used to obtain them — model id, voice, language coverage, and
which channel carried the style. That is enough to re-run the comparison under
your own contract with each vendor.

Our own audio is absent for a duller reason: it is large and fully regenerable
from `prompts/` with `scripts/generate.py`.

`scripts/check_publishable.py` fails the build if any audio file, model weight,
credential, or private path enters the tree.
